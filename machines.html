Our Machines & Equipment

Imaging and laboratory equipment that supports our diagnostic services. Select View Details on any machine to see what it is used for.

Imaging & Radiology Equipment

The appropriate examination depends on your doctor's advice.

CBCT

MYRAY 3D CBCT

Machine
MYRAY 3D CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography)
Purpose
Detailed three-dimensional imaging of the teeth, jaws and surrounding structures.
About this equipment
A CBCT scanner rotates around the head to capture many images, which are combined by a computer into a 3D view that the dentist or doctor can examine from different angles. It is requested when a conventional dental X-ray or a panoramic X-ray does not give enough information, for example when planning dental implants or assessing impacted teeth. Your dentist or doctor decides whether a CBCT scan is needed.

X-Ray

X-Ray Machine

Machine
X-Ray Machine (general diagnostic X-ray)
Purpose
Diagnostic X-ray imaging for appropriate examinations of bones, chest and other body areas.
About this equipment
X-rays pass through the body and are recorded to form an image. Dense structures such as bone appear differently from soft tissue and air-filled lungs, which helps doctors assess the area being examined. The doctor decides which examination is appropriate.

Ultrasound

Voluson P8 Ultrasound

Machine
Voluson P8 Ultrasound system
Purpose
Ultrasound imaging using high-frequency sound waves to examine relevant internal organs and soft tissues.
About this equipment
A hand-held probe is moved over the skin. It sends out sound waves and picks up the echoes, which the system turns into images on the screen. Ultrasound does not use ionising radiation. The type of scan performed depends on what your doctor has prescribed.

X-Ray

FUJI CR Reader

Machine
FUJI CR Reader (computed radiography)
Purpose
Reads X-ray imaging plates and converts the exposure into a digital image.
About this equipment
In computed radiography, the X-ray exposure is captured on an imaging plate. The plate is then placed in the reader, which scans it and produces a digital image for the radiology team and your doctor to review.

Laboratory Equipment

Analysers and laboratory equipment used for sample testing and preparation.

Biochemistry

ERBA 200 Automated Biochemistry Analyser

Machine
ERBA 200 Automated Biochemistry Analyser
Purpose
Automated clinical biochemistry testing of blood and other samples.
About this equipment
A biochemistry analyser measures chemical substances in a sample. The sample is processed automatically, and the tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Biochemistry

FUJI Dry Chemistry Analyser

Machine
FUJI Dry Chemistry Analyser
Purpose
Clinical chemistry testing using dry test slides.
About this equipment
A dry chemistry analyser uses ready-to-use dry test slides instead of liquid reagents to measure chemical substances in a small sample. The tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Haematology

ERBA H560 Haematology Analyser

Machine
ERBA H560 Haematology Analyser
Purpose
Automated blood cell counting and analysis.
About this equipment
A haematology analyser counts and measures the cells in a blood sample, such as red cells, white cells and platelets. These results help doctors assess general health and a range of conditions.

Haematology

SYSMEX XP-100 Cell Counter

Machine
SYSMEX XP-100 Cell Counter
Purpose
Automated blood cell counting.
About this equipment
A cell counter measures the number and size of the cells in a blood sample. The tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Immunoassay

iFlash 1200 Immunoassay Analyser

Machine
iFlash 1200 Immunoassay Analyser
Purpose
Automated immunoassay testing.
About this equipment
An immunoassay analyser measures specific substances in a sample, such as hormones or other markers, using antibody-based reactions. The tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Immunoassay

MINI VIDAS Immunoassay Analyser

Machine
MINI VIDAS Immunoassay Analyser
Purpose
Immunoassay testing on a compact analyser.
About this equipment
An immunoassay analyser measures specific substances in a sample using antibody-based reactions. The tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Electrolytes

Electrolyte Analyser

Machine
Electrolyte Analyser
Purpose
Measurement of electrolytes in a sample.
About this equipment
An electrolyte analyser measures electrolytes, such as sodium and potassium, in a sample. The tests performed are those requested by your doctor.

Laboratory analyser

BIO-RAD D-10 Analyser

Machine
BIO-RAD D-10 Analyser
Purpose
Laboratory analyser used in clinical testing.
About this equipment
The Bio-Rad D-10 is a laboratory analyser. The specific tests performed on it are determined by the laboratory and requested by your doctor.

Sample preparation

NEYA Bench-top Centrifuge

Machine
NEYA Bench-top Centrifuge
Purpose
Separating the components of a sample before testing.
About this equipment
A centrifuge spins samples at high speed so that their components separate, for example blood cells from serum or plasma, before the sample is tested.
