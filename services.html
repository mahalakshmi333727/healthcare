Diagnostic Services

Imaging, laboratory, cardiac, hearing, eye testing and counselling services at Harmony Diagnostic, Pallikaranai. Select a service below for details. The appropriate examination depends on your doctor's advice.

Imaging & Scan Services

The information below is general guidance. Availability, timings and preparation for each examination can be confirmed with our team.

MRI scanning uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves, rather than X-rays, to produce detailed cross-sectional images of structures inside the body. It supports the assessment of the brain, spine, joints, soft tissues and other areas, based on the referring doctor's recommendation.

Clinical Applications

  • Assessment of the brain and spine
  • Evaluation of joints, muscles and ligaments
  • Examination of soft tissues and selected internal organs

Patient Information

The body area to be scanned depends on your doctor's referral, so please bring the referral with you. Inform our team beforehand about any metal implants, pacemakers or similar devices, as these can affect MRI safety. MRI availability and scheduling should be confirmed with our team before you book.

Enquire About MRI

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is a three-dimensional imaging technique for the teeth and jaws. The scanner rotates around the head and captures multiple images, which are combined into a 3D view of the teeth, jawbones and surrounding structures that the dentist or doctor can examine from different angles. Scans are performed on our MYRAY 3D CBCT system.

Clinical Applications

  • Planning dental implants
  • Assessment of impacted teeth
  • Other dental and jaw examinations where a 3D view is needed

Dental Imaging Options Compared

Dental X-ray, OPG and CBCT each serve a different purpose, and one does not replace the others.

Comparison of dental X-ray, OPG and CBCT imaging
FeatureDental X-rayOPGCBCT
Type of image2D, close-up2D, panoramic3D
Area shownA small area, usually a few teeth at a timeUpper and lower jaws and teeth in one imageTeeth, jaws and relevant surrounding structures, viewable from different angles
Commonly used forCavities, roots and the structures supporting the teethAn overview of the teeth and jaws, including impacted teethDental implant planning, impacted teeth and other cases where 3D detail is needed

Patient Information

Your dentist or doctor decides which type of imaging is appropriate, so please bring the request with you. CBCT uses X-rays; tell our team if you are, or may be, pregnant before the scan.

Enquire About CBCT

An Orthopantomogram (OPG), also called a panoramic dental X-ray, captures the upper and lower jaws and the teeth in a single wide image. The machine moves around the head during the scan.

Clinical Applications

  • Assessment of impacted teeth
  • General evaluation of the teeth and jaws
  • An overview of the teeth and jaws as part of dental assessments

Patient Information

An OPG is a flat, two-dimensional image. It does not show the fine detail of individual teeth in the way a close-up dental X-ray does, and it does not give a 3D view. If more detail is needed, your dentist or doctor may advise a dental X-ray or a CBCT scan. OPG uses X-rays; tell our team if you are, or may be, pregnant before the scan.

Enquire About OPG

General X-ray imaging passes a small, controlled amount of X-ray radiation through the body to record an image. Dense structures such as bone appear differently from soft tissue and air-filled lungs, which helps doctors assess the area being examined.

Clinical Applications

  • Chest imaging
  • Bone imaging
  • Other examinations requested by your doctor, subject to the services available at the facility

Patient Information

Your doctor determines the appropriate examination, so please bring the request with you. Tell our team if you are, or may be, pregnant before the X-ray. Our team can confirm which examinations are available.

Enquire About X-ray

A dental X-ray is a close-up image of the teeth and the structures around them. It helps dentists assess the teeth, the roots, and the bone and tissues that support them.

Clinical Applications

  • Detection of cavities, including those not visible on direct examination of the tooth
  • Assessment of the roots and the structures supporting the teeth
  • Assessment of other dental problems, where appropriate

Patient Information

Your dentist decides when a dental X-ray is appropriate. For a broader view of both jaws, an OPG may be advised, and for 3D detail, a CBCT scan. Tell our team if you are, or may be, pregnant before the X-ray.

Enquire About Dental X-ray

Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves to create images of structures inside the body. A hand-held probe is moved over the skin, and the returning echoes are displayed as images on a screen. Ultrasound does not use ionising radiation. Scans are performed on our Voluson P8 ultrasound system.

Clinical Applications

  • Examination of relevant organs and soft tissues
  • Imaging of other areas of the body, depending on the scan prescribed

Patient Information

The examination is chosen according to your doctor's prescription, and any preparation depends on the scan. Please confirm the range of ultrasound examinations available with our team before you book.

Enquire About Ultrasound

Cardiac diagnostics at Harmony Diagnostic include electrocardiography (ECG) and echocardiography (Echo), which help doctors assess the heart.

Cardiac Tests

  • Echocardiography (Echo): uses ultrasound to create images of the heart's structure and movement

Patient Information
